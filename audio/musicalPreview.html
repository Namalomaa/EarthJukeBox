What the data controls

Flash count → rhythmic density · Latitude → melody pitch · Longitude → spatial interpretation for later stereo work · Radiance → melody intensity. A consistent C-major/A-minor harmonic progression keeps the result musical.

Important: This prototype compresses your March 1–12, 2017 observations into 30 seconds. It is not synchronized to the complete NASA 2017–2023 movie. This is intentional: we are validating the musical approach first.
