Scientific audit / mapping explanation

Source: data/processed/lightning.json (ISS LIS V3, actual flashes). Input count: —. A 30-second composition compresses the entire available ~11-day observation span into 15 successive sections. Counts are detected flashes, not global flash rates. Each audible melody note is selected from a real observation: latitude → pentatonic pitch, radiance → loudness, longitude → map position, section flash count → melodic density. Harmonizing chords, bass and soft percussion are a fixed musical scaffold, not NASA measurements.
